## Project principles

- Keep public tasks easy to find and understand.
- Use plain language and clear action labels.
- Preserve responsive behavior on desktop and mobile.
- Do not publish unverified contact information or legal guidance.
- Keep the WordPress theme and static export synchronized when both are changed.
