# Site Overview

## Primary sections

- About: Meet the Sheriff, Mission and Values, and Organization
- Divisions: Enforcement, Judicial, Court Services, and Jail content
- Services: Reports, public safety, permits, and court-service fees
- Resources: Common public services and safety information
- Contact: Office contact information and forms

## Organization hierarchy

The Organization page presents the hierarchy visually:

```text
Sheriff
└── Chief Deputy
    ├── Enforcement Bureau
    └── Judicial Bureau
```

Division names appear beneath their bureau cards. The connector lines are visual aids; the content must remain understandable when styles are unavailable.

## Public Records

The site presents Public Records as an informational notice. The verified wording identifies Captain Chuck Kirby as the Gordon County Sheriff’s Office Custodian of Records. Do not add a request form, direct email, or personal phone number unless it has been verified by the Sheriff’s Office.

## Court Services Fees

Fees are listed for general information and may change. Confirm current fees and payment requirements before publishing updates.
