# Accessibility Checklist

Before publishing a page, verify:

- A single meaningful page title is present.
- Heading levels follow a logical hierarchy.
- Links describe their destination.
- Keyboard focus is visible.
- Interactive targets are large enough for touch use.
- Text and controls have sufficient color contrast.
- Images have useful alternative text or are marked decorative.
- Information is not communicated by color alone.
- External links identify that a new site or window will open.
- The page remains usable at mobile widths and with zoom.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected where animation is present.

For org charts and visual layouts, ensure the underlying text and reading order still communicate the hierarchy without relying on connector lines.
